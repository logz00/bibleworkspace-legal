Privacy Policy

BibleStudy · Last updated 15 August 2026

BibleStudy is a Bible reading and study app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what control you have over it. If you have questions, contact us at bibleworkspace@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to function. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

WhatWhy
Email addressTo create and secure your account, and to send password resets. Stored privately and never shown to other users.
Display name and usernameShown on your profile so friends can find you.
Profile photo and bio (optional)Shown on your profile. Only stored if you add them.
Your study content — notes, highlights, bookmarks, tags, reading progressTo save your work and sync it across your devices.
Friends, friend requests, and shared workspace membershipTo provide the social and collaboration features.
Messages you send in shared workspacesTo deliver them to the other members of that workspace.
App preferences — theme, font, layout, default translationTo keep the app looking the way you set it.
In-app coins and cosmetic itemsTo track items earned in the app. These have no cash value and cannot be purchased with real money.
Reports and blocks you submitTo review reported content and enforce our content policy.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data, browsing history, or device advertising identifiers.

Who can see your information

  • Your email address is private. It is never visible to other users.
  • Your profile — name, username, photo, bio, join date, and coin balance — is visible to other signed-in users, including through user search.
  • Your notes, highlights, and bookmarks are private unless you put them in a shared workspace, in which case the other members of that workspace can see them.
  • Messages in a shared workspace are visible to the members of that workspace.
  • Blocks are private. A user is not told when they have been blocked.

Service providers

Your account and content are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes this data on our behalf.

Bible text is retrieved from third-party sources — bolls.life and a public content delivery network. These requests ask only for the passage you are reading; no account information is sent with them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This removes your profile, notes, bookmarks, workspaces, friendships, and login credentials. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Messages you sent in a shared workspace may remain visible to that workspace's other members after deletion, since they form part of their conversation history. You can delete your own messages before deleting your account if you want them removed.

Reports submitted about other users are retained after account deletion so that safety enforcement is not defeated by deleting an account.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed as described above. Backups maintained by our service provider may persist for a short period before being overwritten in the normal course of operation.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access rules that restrict each user's private content to that user. No online service can guarantee perfect security, but we limit what we collect and who can read it.

Children

BibleStudy is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can access and correct your profile information in the app, and delete everything through Settings → Delete Account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to request a copy of your data or its deletion — email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
