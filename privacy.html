Privacy Policy

BibleStudy · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy covers what BibleStudy stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. Questions go to bibleworkspace@gmail.com.

What we store

Only what the app needs to work. No ads, no analytics, no tracking you across other apps or websites.

WhatWhy
Email addressTo create your account and send password resets. Kept private and never shown to other users.
Display name and usernameShown on your profile so friends can find you.
Profile photo and bio (optional)Shown on your profile. Only stored if you add them.
Your study content: notebook pages, notes, highlights, bookmarks, tags, reading plans, reading progress, and where you are readingTo save your work and sync it across your devices.
Friends, friend requests, and shared workspace membershipTo run the social and collaboration features.
Messages you send in shared workspacesTo deliver them to the other members of that workspace.
Direct messages you send to friends, including verses and workspace invites you shareTo deliver them to that friend.
A notification address for your phone, if you turn notifications onTo tell you about new messages, friend requests and workspace invites. Sent through Expo's push service and Apple or Google. Turn it off in Settings at any time.
Whether you have the app open (online status)So friends and workspace members can see who is around. Only an on/off flag and the time it was last updated.
App preferences: theme, font, layout, default translationTo keep the app looking the way you set it.
In-app coins and cosmetic itemsTo track what you have earned. These have no cash value and cannot be bought with real money.
Reports and blocks you submitTo review reported content and act on it.

Reading without an account. You can read the Bible without signing in. Then nothing is sent to us: your place in the reader and your appearance settings stay on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

Who can see it

  • Your email is private. Other users never see it.
  • Your profile is public to signed-in users. That means your name, username, photo, bio, join date, coin balance, and the profile border and badge you have chosen, including in user search.
  • Your notebook, reading progress, plans, place in the Bible, and settings are private. Only you can see them.
  • Your notes, highlights, and bookmarks are private unless you put them in a shared workspace. Then the other members of that workspace can see them.
  • Messages in a shared workspace are visible to that workspace's members.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the friend you are talking to. You can only message people on your friends list, and the conversation closes if either of you removes or blocks the other.
  • Your online status can be seen by other signed-in users. The app shows it to your friends and to members of workspaces you share.
  • Blocks are private. Nobody is told they have been blocked.

Who else is involved

Your account and content live in Google Firebase, which handles authentication and storage for us.

The Bible text is built into the app, so reading it sends nothing to anyone. The translations come from eBible.org; the app lists each one and its licence under Settings, then Sources and licences.

We do not sell your information or hand it to advertisers or data brokers.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings, then Delete Account. This wipes your profile, notes, bookmarks, plans, progress, workspaces, friendships, online status, and login. Messages you sent, in direct messages and in shared workspaces, are deleted too, and your direct conversations are closed. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Reports filed about other users are kept, so that deleting an account cannot wipe out a safety record.

How long we keep things

As long as your account exists. After you delete it, your data goes as described above. Provider backups may hold copies briefly before they are overwritten.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules keep your private content readable only by you. No service can promise perfect security, so we keep collection to a minimum.

Children

BibleStudy is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly store their information. Tell us if a child under 13 has an account and we will delete it.

Your rights

Edit your profile in the app, and delete everything through Settings. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to request a copy of your data. Email us and we will sort it out.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes too, and we will say so in the app where it makes sense.
